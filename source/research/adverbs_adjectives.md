# Adverbs and Descriptions Lane Research

Purpose: convert the saved Ekaterina guide's adverb/adjective grammar tables
into short oral survival lines Joe can actually use during family conversation.

## Source-guide anchors

- `source/ekaterina_guide.md:471-472`
  - adverb list includes practical answer/repair words such as `интере́сно`,
    `тру́дно`, `легко́`, `бы́стро`, `ме́дленно`, `ва́жно`, and `удо́бно`.
- `source/ekaterina_guide.md:476-554`
  - `не` with adverbs, supporting compact negated lines like `не тру́дно`,
    `не о́чень удо́бно`, and `не ва́жно`.
- `source/ekaterina_guide.md:1088-1099`
  - contrast between adverbs (`Как?`) and feminine adjective agreement with
    `пого́да`: `со́лнечная`, `дождли́вая`, `хоро́шая`, `пло́хая`.
- `source/ekaterina_guide.md:1917-1935`
  - homework directs Joe to make and review adverb lists and answer adverb
    exercises.

## Added phrase decisions

- The module favors short evaluative sentences: `Э́то ...` plus one pace repair
  line, because those are usable under real conversation pressure.
- Weather adjective cards duplicate the guide's grammar contrast intentionally:
  Joe already has `Сего́дня со́лнечно`; this lane teaches the feminine pattern
  `Пого́да со́лнечная`.
- The lane avoids spelling-lesson detail about when `не` is one word or two
  words; the app goal is oral survival, not written grammar.

## Verification notes

- Stress marks use combining acute. `ё` marks stress when present.
- The selected items are source-guide vocabulary recombined into short oral
  patterns; no new idiomatic Russian is introduced here.
