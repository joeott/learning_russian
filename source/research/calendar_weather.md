## External checks

- Learn Russian Step by Step, "Talking about the weather":
  `https://learnrussianstepbystep.com/en/talking-about-the-weather/`
  - Confirms: `Кака́я сего́дня пого́да?`, `Как там на у́лице?`,
    `Идёт дождь.`, `Идёт снег.`, `Хо́лодно.`, `Тепло́.`, `Со́лнечно.`,
    `Вчера́ бы́ло со́лнечно.`, `Завтра будет тепло́.`
- Learn Russian Step by Step, "Days of the week" lesson variants:
  `https://learnrussianstepbystep.com/fr/les-jours-de-la-semaine-en-russe/`
  - Confirms stressed day names:
    `понеде́льник`, `вто́рник`, `среда́`, `четве́рг`, `пя́тница`,
    `суббо́та`, `воскресе́нье`.
  - Confirms accusative "on [day]" pattern such as `в понеде́льник`,
    `во вто́рник`, `в сре́ду`, `в четве́рг`, `в пя́тницу`.
- Russian Wiktionary, `о́блачно`:
  `https://ru.wiktionary.org/wiki/облачно`
  - Confirms first-syllable stress and adverb status.

## Course decisions

- Use mostly short whole-phrase answers, not grammar tables.
- Mark weather questions as recognition-first because relatives are likely to ask
  them faster than Joe can produce them.
- Keep the seven day names as bonus/recognition vocabulary because the original
  guide repeatedly drills day-of-week prompts.
