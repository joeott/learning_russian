## Web checks

- RussianForFree, "Possessive pronouns":
  https://www.russianforfree.com/grammar-of-russian-possessive-pronouns.php
  - Confirms that Russian possessive pronouns agree with the owned noun by
    gender/number/case (`мой`, `моя`, `моё`, `мои`).
- RussianLessons.net, "Russian Pronouns":
  https://www.russianlessons.net/grammar/pronouns.php
  - Cross-checks personal pronouns and possessive forms, including third-person
    `его` / `её`.
- Wikibooks, "Russian/Personal Pronouns":
  https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Russian/Personal_Pronouns
  - Confirms the `н-` prefix after prepositions for third-person pronouns,
    e.g. `у него`.

## Added course lane

Added `pronouns_possession` with 16 oral cards:

- possessives by noun shape: `мой дом`, `моя жена`, `моё любимое место`, `мои
  коты`, `моих котов`, `моя визитка`
- third-person/formal possessives: `его дом`, `её машина`, `наши коты`,
  `ваш ключ`
- `у` possession/contact patterns: `У меня есть два кота`, `У нас было много
  гостей`, `У вас есть время?`
- high-value relationship/work phrases: `Моя жена Кадрия работает вместе со
  мной`, `Они работают вместе`, `Я соглашусь с тобой`

This keeps the source-guide pronoun material oral and useful for family-table
and travel situations.
