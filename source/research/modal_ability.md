## Web verification

- Ruspeach explains the practical beginner distinction: `мочь` is ability or
  possibility, while `уметь` is a learned skill.
  https://www.ruspeach.com/en/learning/13929/
- Easy Russian covers modal predicatives like `можно`, `нельзя`, `надо`, and
  `нужно` for possibility, prohibition, and necessity.
  https://easy-russian.com/modal-predicatives/

## Course decisions

- Keep this as a dedicated `modal_ability` module because the forms appear
  across work, food, hobbies, travel, and repair contexts.
- Use source-guide dance/salsa examples as low-pressure skill examples, not
  mission-critical production claims.
- Keep permission/necessity prompts recognition-first where they are likely to
  be heard as instructions.
- Mark the `должен готовить чаще` card as `rehearse=True` because it is a
  personal family joke.
