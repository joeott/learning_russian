# Meeting your Russian in-laws

Do you have a Russian girlfriend or boyfriend? Are you preparing to meet the parents of your darling for the first time? If so, this lesson is for you!

Today we will [learn Russian](/en/learn-russian/) words and phrases that you may need when meeting Russian family of your beloved one. Learn these simple lines by heart, and you are sure to make a good impression on your future in-laws! 🙂

Здра́вствуйте.  
[sdrástvuytye]  
*Hello.*  
{formal}

Приве́т.  
[preevyét]  
*Hi.*  
{informal}

Пожа́луйста.  
[pazhálusta]  
*Please. / You’re welcome.*

Спаси́бо.  
[spaséeba]  
*Thank you.*

Спаси́бо большо́е.  
[spaséeba bal’shóye]  
*Thank you very much.*

Прия́тно познако́миться.  
[preeyátna paznakómeetsa]  
*Nice to meet you. (Pleasure to meet you.)*

О́чень прия́тно.  
[óchyen’ preeyátna]  
*Nice to meet you. (Very pleasurable.)*

Мо́жно?  
[mózhna]  
*Can I? May I?*

Мо́жно ча́ю?  
[mózhna cháyu]  
*Can I have a cup of tea?*

[![Russian Pod 101]()](https://www.russianpod101.com/member/go.php?r=75089&l=uggcf%3A%2F%2Fjjj.ehffvnacbq101.pbz%2Fernqlfrgfcrnx-yc)

Ещё.  
[eeschyó]  
*More.*

Мо́жно ещё ко́фе?  
[mózhna eeschyó kófee]  
*Can I have another coffee?*

Вку́сно.  
[fkúsna]  
*Tasty.*

О́чень вку́сно.  
[óchyen’ fkúsna]  
*Very tasty.*

Пото́м.  
[patóm]  
*Later.*

По́зже.  
[pózhe]  
*Later.*

Уже́.  
[uzhé]  
*Already.*

До́брое у́тро.  
[dóbraye útra]  
*Good morning.*

До́брый день.  
[dóbruiy dyen’]   
*Good afternoon.*

До́брый ве́чер.  
[dóbruiy vyecher]  
*Good evening.*

До́брой но́чи.  
[dóbray nóchee]  
*Good night.*

Поня́тно.  
[panyátna]  
*Understood. Clear. I see.*

Не поня́тно.  
[nye panyátna]  
*Not understood.*

Понима́ю.  
[paneemáyu]  
*I understand.*

Не понима́ю.  
[nye paneemáyu]  
*I don’t understand.*

Понима́ете?  
[paneemáeetye]  
*Do you understand?*   
(formal)

[![blank](https://learnrussianstepbystep.com/wp-content/plugins/wp-fastest-cache-premium/pro/images/blank.gif)](https://lingopie.com/?tap_a=105960-b87f6a&ref=zjk2ytz)[![blank](https://learnrussianstepbystep.com/wp-content/plugins/wp-fastest-cache-premium/pro/images/blank.gif)](https://lingopie.com/?tap_a=105955-b7c625&ref=zjk2ytz)

Понима́ешь?  
[paneemáeesh]  
*Do you understand?*   
(informal)

Нет, спаси́бо.  
[nyet spaséeba]  
*No, thanks.*

Спаси́бо, не на́до.  
[spaséeba nye náda]  
*Thanks, no need.*

Мне нра́вится.  
[mnye nráveetsa]  
*I like.*

Мне не нра́вится.  
[mnye nye nráveetsa]  
*I don’t like.*

Прия́тного аппети́та.  
[preeyátnava apeetéeta]  
*Bon appetite.*

Прости́те.  
[prastéetee]  
*Pardon me. Sorry.*   
{formal}

Прости́.  
[prastée]  
*Pardon me. Sorry.*   
{informal}

Извини́те.  
[eezveenéetee]  
*Sorry.*  
{formal}

Извини́.  
[eezveenée]  
*Sorry.*   
{informal}

Замеча́тельно.  
[zameecháteel’na]   
*Wonderful.*

Отли́чно.  
[atléechna]  
*Great.*

Хорошо́.  
[harashó]  
*Good. Agree.*

Бу́дьте здоро́вы.  
[but’tye sdaróvy]   
*Bless you. (Be healthy.)*   
{after sneezing, formal}

Будь здоро́в.  
[but’ sdaróf]  
*Bless you. (Be healthy.)*   
{after sneezing, informal, masculin}

Будь здоро́ва.  
[but’ sdaróva]  
*Bless you. (Be healthy.)*   
{after sneezing, informal, feminine}

На здоровье.  
[nа sdaróvye]  
*You are welcome. / For the health.*  
{a response to ‘thank you for the meal’ / a toast}
