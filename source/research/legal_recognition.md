## Added phrase decisions

- All cards are `recognize=True` and priority 3. They are for understanding,
  not for Joe initiating legal explanations.
- The lane keeps the vocabulary short and concrete: court, judge, hearing, case,
  client, insurance, settlement, lawsuit, injury, defamation, law/right,
  prosecutor, defender, and summons.
- High-risk narrative sentences about accusations, jail, insurance threats, and
  police reports are intentionally not production cards.

## Verification notes

- Stress marks use combining acute. One-syllable words such as `суд` do not need
  an acute mark.
- `мирно́е соглаше́ние` is used for settlement agreement because the guide later
  gives `мирное соглашение` as settlement.
