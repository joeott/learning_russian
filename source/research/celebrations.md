small talk without making these lower-priority topics too complex for a
beginner dinner conversation.

## Added phrase decisions

- This module is priority 3 because it improves original-guide coverage, but the
  June family visit is better served by table, repair, work, routine, and Budva
  phrases first.
- Several prompts are recognition-only so Joe can understand family questions
  without needing to initiate holiday talk.
- The Oregon/Christmas sentences are retained because they are in the guide and
  train future-plan patterns (`мы хоти́м`, `мы бу́дем`), even though they are not
  directly mission-critical for Budva.

## Verification notes

- Stress marks use combining acute. `Рождество́`, `О́регон`, `О́регоне`,
  `Благодаре́ния`, and `семьёй` are marked for learner-facing cards.
- Long cultural/legal explanations from the source were not promoted to
  production cards; they are too brittle for beginner small talk.
