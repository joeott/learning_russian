## Added phrase decisions

- The lane keeps `Я юри́ст` in `smalltalk` as the main job identity and adds
  follow-up phrases for clients, cases, company, court, and being busy.
- Questions use formal `вы` where Joe may hear them from elders.
- Past-tense first-person masculine phrases are marked `gender="m"` because Joe
  should say the masculine form.
- Deeper legal vocabulary such as bankruptcy, criminal accusations, settlement,
  and insurance threats is not promoted to core beginner production because it
  would invite overexplaining and high-risk errors.

## Verification notes

- Stress marks use combining acute. `клие́нт`, `суде́`, `результа́том`, and
  `себя́` are marked for the learner-facing cards.
- `дел` is the genitive plural of `де́ло` after `много`; the spoken pattern is
  intentionally limited to `мно́го дел`.
