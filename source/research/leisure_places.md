## Web verification

- Russian Language Stack Exchange summarizes the beginner distinction: use
  `играть на` for musical instruments and `играть в` for games/sports.
  https://russian.stackexchange.com/questions/27216/play-vs-play-on
- RussianForFree gives the same contrast with examples `играть на гитаре` and
  `играть в футбол`.
  https://www.russianforfree.com/video-with-anastasia-to-play.php

## Course decisions

- Add a dedicated `leisure_places` module because this material is broad enough
  to be a natural dinner conversation lane, not only a daily-routine footnote.
- Keep place nouns like `музе́й`, `стадио́н`, and `кинотеа́тр` recognition-first
  where they are mostly useful for understanding prompts.
- Include notes on `игра́ть на` vs `игра́ть в` because this is a high-frequency
  beginner error and the original guide has both instruments and sports.
