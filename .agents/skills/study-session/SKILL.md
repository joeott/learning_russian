# Study-session methodology

Goal: **oral readiness** for a St. Petersburg family dinner (June 15). Optimize
for being understood and understanding — not perfection. Joe reads Cyrillic; his
bottleneck is **word stress / vowel reduction** and **speaking under pressure**.

## Evidence-based moves (apply every session)
- **Retrieval practice** beats re-reading — make him produce, don't just show.
- **Spaced repetition** — clear Anki daily; revisit yesterday's misses first.
- **Comprehensible input + audio** — play the native MP3 (web app / `tools/zastolom`),
  shadow it aloud. Audio is primary for an oral goal.
- **Interleave** modules once basics are in; **risk-first** ordering otherwise.
- **Desirable difficulty** — climb the ladder; don't stay on recognition.

## The difficulty ladder
recognition → cued recall (EN→RU) → production (say/type) → listening (audio→meaning)
→ unscripted role-play. The web app's Drill mirrors stages 1–5; the tutor handles 5.

## 10-minute day (busy weekday)
1. Clear Anki due cards (protect the spacing curve).
2. 2 min: shadow 5 P1 audio clips aloud (stress focus).
3. 3 min: one Drill round (recognition/recall).
No new material on 10-minute days — protect the foundation.

## Weekend session (30–45 min)
1. Warm-up: yesterday's misses (5 min).
2. New module from the schedule — Learn + audio shadowing (10 min).
3. Production + listening Drill (10 min).
4. Role-play with the tutor: doorway → intro → food/compliment → toast (10–15 min).
5. Close: 3 wins, 2 to drill, items to rehearse with Kadriya.

## Pronunciation focus (the high-leverage fix)
Mark stress (ударе́ние); have him repeat; target unstressed о→[a], е→[i], and
-ого/-его → spoken [-ova]/[-eva]. Compare his attempt to the native MP3.

## Guardrails
Only practice Russian that's in `content/content.json` (or verified). Address
elders with **вы + name & patronymic**. «За здоро́вье!» is the toast (never «На
здоровье»). Hold male forms (рад, нае́лся).
